If a learner using this platform is under 18, a parent or guardian should be aware of and consent to their use, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), which treats anyone under 18 as a child and generally requires verifiable parental consent before processing a child's personal data. UNIC Academic does not knowingly collect more personal information from a minor than is needed to show them content and record their own progress.
