Become an Editor

Write chapters, add questions, and see your work go live

As an editor, you get your own workspace inside the Question Builder for the subjects you're assigned to. Here's what that means in practice:

  • Write the theory. Build out a chapter's concept using headings, paragraphs, examples, tables, images, and videos — however the subject needs it explained.
  • Add practice questions. MCQ, MSQ, numeric-answer, assertion-reason, or match-the-following — pick whichever fits what you're testing.
  • Organize it your way. Structure content as Units → Chapters, or relabel that to Weeks → Days, Modules → Lessons — whatever matches your subject.
  • Import from a template. Draft offline in your own spreadsheet or editor, then upload it in one go instead of typing everything by hand.
  • Preview before you publish. See exactly how a chapter will look to a learner before you save it.
  • Get credit for your work. Every save is logged under your name, so your contributions are always traceable back to you.

Once approved, you'll only see and edit the subject(s) mapped to your account — nothing else is cluttering your workspace.
