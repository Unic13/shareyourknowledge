📝 General Admission Procedure
A typical path from exam to enrollment — exact steps vary by institution and exam
1
Register & apply on the official portal
Create an account on the exam body's site (e.g. NTA, UPSC, SSC), fill the application form, and pay the fee before the deadline.
2
Download the admit card
Usually released a few weeks before the exam date, from the same official portal — never from a third-party site.
3
Appear for the exam
Written test, sometimes followed by an interview, group discussion, or skill/physical test depending on the exam.
4
Result & rank/merit list
Scores and All-India ranks are published on the official site; category-wise cutoffs determine eligibility for counseling.
5
Counseling & seat allotment
Register for counseling (e.g. JoSAA for JEE, MCC for NEET), fill college/course choices in order of preference, and get seats allotted round by round.
6
Document verification & fee payment
Report to the allotted institution (or online reporting portal) with original documents, pay admission fees, and confirm your seat.
